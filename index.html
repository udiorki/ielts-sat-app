Georgia O'Keeffe

For seven decades, Georgia O'Keeffe (1887-1986) was a major figure in American art. Remarkably, she remained independent from European trends, carving out her own unique style.

Her early work focused on dramatic, large-scale flowers and urban landscapes, which instantly captured public attention.
